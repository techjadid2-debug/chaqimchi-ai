Frequently asked questions

Have questions?

Can I install it on my existing PC?

Yes. A PC running Windows 10/11 is enough: download the app, Next → Next → Done, then connect the cameras in the browser. If your NVR is already on the network, the whole process usually takes up to 30 minutes. If the NVR needs setting up too — 45–90 minutes with a technician. Step-by-step guide.

What if I do not have a PC?

Leave your number — we will suggest an option for your case: adapt what you have or install a ready device.

Do I have to replace my cameras?

Usually not. If your camera or NVR provides an RTSP stream on the local network, the system works with it. Tested devices: Hikvision, HiLook, Dahua and Uniview NVRs. Other brands may work too — we check for free before connecting.

Where is the video stored?

The full video archive stays on your own NVR or PC. Only event statistics and short clips are sent to the cloud, encrypted.

Which plan should I choose?

One till and one entrance — Starter is enough: you see how many people came in and out. Need queues, security and the shop map — Business. More than two shops — ask about Network. You can change plans later.

How do I pay? What if I cancel?

Right now you receive an invoice and an operator confirms the payment. Cash or bank transfer. No long-term contract. Once online providers are connected, the panel shows only the working method. Full refund within 14 days of the first payment — terms in the public offer.
